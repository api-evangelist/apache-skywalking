This is the second post in the Apache SkyWalking Horizon UI series. The first one introduced the console and its layered navigation; this one is about the surface you spend most of your day on — the dashboards . Every dashboard in Horizon is the same machine: a grid of widgets, each one an MQE expression the BFF resolves against OAP.
