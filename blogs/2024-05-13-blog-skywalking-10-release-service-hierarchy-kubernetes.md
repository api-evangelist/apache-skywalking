The Apache SkyWalking team today announced the 10 release. SkyWalking 10 provides a host of groundbreaking features and enhancements. The introduction of Layer and Service Hierarchy streamlines monitoring by organizing services and metrics into distinct layers and providing seamless navigation across them.
