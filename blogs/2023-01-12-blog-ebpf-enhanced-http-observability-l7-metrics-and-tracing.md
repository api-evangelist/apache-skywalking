Background Apache SkyWalking is an open-source Application Performance Management system that helps users collect and aggregate logs, traces, metrics, and events for display on a UI. In the previous article , we introduced how to use Apache SkyWalking Rover to analyze the network performance issue in the service mesh environment. However, in business scenarios, users often rely on mature layer 7 protocols, such as HTTP, for interactions between systems.
