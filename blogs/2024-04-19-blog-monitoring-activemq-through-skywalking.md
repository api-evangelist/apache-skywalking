Introduction Apache ActiveMQ Classic is a popular and powerful open-source messaging and integration pattern server. Founded in 2004, it has evolved into a mature and widely used open-source messaging middleware that complies with the Java Message Service (JMS) . Today, with its stability and wide range of feature support, it still has a certain number of users of small and medium-sized enterprises.
